BumpWell Privacy Policy

BumpWell: Pregnancy & Baby · Effective 16 October 2026

In short
  • Everything you record in BumpWell (your pregnancy, health readings, your baby's growth) is stored only on your phone. We have no servers and we cannot see it.
  • BumpWell is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. The ads are non-personalized, and we never give Google, or anyone else, any of your health or baby data.
  • Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to share a report or a backup file.

This policy explains what information BumpWell handles, where it is kept and what choices you have. "We" and "us" means Bilal Azmat, the developer of BumpWell.

1. Information you enter, kept on your phone

To track your pregnancy and your baby, you may enter:

  • Profile: name, date of birth, due date, last period date, cycle length, number of babies expected, birth date, height, pre-pregnancy weight, blood group, previous pregnancies and births, health conditions, and your care team's name and phone number.
  • Daily logs: mood, symptoms, temperature, sleep, water, periods and notes.
  • Measurements and counters: your weight, your baby's weight, length and head size, kick counts and contraction times.
  • Health readings: blood pressure, blood glucose, exercise and hydration.
  • Care: appointments, medicines and supplements with their reminders, and questions for your doctor.

This information is stored in a database inside the app on your phone. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is not used for advertising. We do not run analytics on it.

2. How your data is protected

  • App lock: you can lock BumpWell with your phone's lock (fingerprint, face or screen lock) or a 4-digit BumpWell PIN. The PIN is never stored itself; only a salted, one-way hash of it is kept on your phone.
  • No automatic cloud copy: Android's automatic backup to a Google account is turned off for BumpWell, so your health data is never copied off your phone without you doing it.
  • Anyone who can unlock your phone and BumpWell can see your data, so we recommend using a screen lock.

3. Data you choose to share

  • Reports: you can create a PDF or HTML report and share it, for example with your doctor. It goes only where you send it.
  • Backup files: you can save a backup file and send it wherever you choose (for example to your own email or cloud storage) to move to a new phone. Backup files are not encrypted, so please keep them somewhere private.

Once you share a file, the app or service you share it with handles it under its own privacy policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

BumpWell shows ads provided by Google AdMob on reading screens such as Articles, Guide, Meal plan, Questions, Trends and Help. Ads are never shown during tracking, on medical alerts, or on the kick counter or contraction timer.

What we do:

  • We request non-personalized ads only. They are not based on your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • We do not send Google any keywords, page content or anything you have entered in BumpWell. Google does not know that you are pregnant or anything about your health from BumpWell.
  • We have turned off Android's ad "Topics" feature for BumpWell, so it is not used to choose BumpWell's ads.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

What Google collects when an ad is shown: when you are online, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be worked out), device model and operating system, app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ads and prevent fraud, even for non-personalized ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), BumpWell asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can watch a short ad to use BumpWell without ads for 24 hours.
  • When your phone is offline, no ads are requested and every feature still works.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can erase everything at once with Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted. Keep a backup file if you want a copy.
  • Information collected by Google for ads is kept under Google's own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or US state laws such as California's), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You can do all of this directly in the app, because your data is only on your phone: edit or delete any entry, export a backup or report, or delete all data. You can withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy options. For information Google collects for ads, see Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for personalized (cross-context behavioural) advertising.

8. Children

BumpWell is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Information you record about your baby is entered by you as a parent and, like all your data, stays on your phone.

9. Health information

BumpWell is for information and personal tracking. It is not a medical device and does not give diagnosis or treatment advice. Always follow your doctor's or midwife's advice, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
